# SYCLE Evidence Curation SOP

## Minimum record before publish
- Exact title, authors, year, publisher/journal, DOI or official URL when available.
- Original PDF or official source document.
- Source type and evidence level.
- One or more claims written in neutral language.
- Population/context clearly stated.
- Limitations explicitly recorded.
- Citation page/section captured when a PDF is used.
- Reviewer approves claim and source.
- At least one claim is reviewed/approved with a citation.
- `evidence_sources.verified = true` and `status = 'published'` only after review.

## Safety rules
- Do not convert association into causation.
- Do not infer individual diagnosis from population research.
- Do not invent thresholds, doses, or treatment recommendations.
- Keep “what is known” separate from “what remains uncertain”.
- AI explanations must cite the retrieved evidence and preserve uncertainty.

## Prototype seeds
The initial demo topics are intentionally scaffold records. They are not a substitute for the exact underlying research documents.
